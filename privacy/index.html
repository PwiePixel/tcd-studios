The following gives a simple overview of what kind of personal information we collect, why we collect them and how we handle your data when you are visiting or using our website. The data collected on this website are processed by the website operator. The operator's contact details can be found in the website's required legal notice.

How do we collect your data?

Some data are collected when you provide them to us. This could, for example, be data you enter in a contact form.

Part of the data is collected to ensure the proper functioning of the website. Other data can be used to analyze how visitors use the site.

When visiting our website, statistical analyses may be made of your surfing behavior. This happens primarily using cookies and analytics. Encrypted identity form and the ID-card photo

To prove your identity and address you enter your name and address in the customer portal and upload a photo of your ID card (front and back) through an encrypted form. We handle the photo as follows:

  • The photo is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection. On arrival it is checked and saved again (this removes metadata such as location or camera data) and is encrypted immediately with a public key (AES-256-GCM, with the key secured by RSA). The matching private key is not stored on any server. Therefore the web server cannot read the photo after the upload. Only during the upload request itself the image briefly exists in unencrypted form in the working memory and in the temporary upload file of the web server.
  • Authorised staff open the encrypted photo in their own browser with the private key and compare it with the address you entered. The photo is decrypted in the browser of the staff member, not on our server. Access to ID-card photos is logged.
  • After the check the photo is deleted from the web server. It is kept only in encrypted form on a separate, access-restricted secure storage system until the end of the service.
  • The photo is used only for the purposes named above. It is never published.

Our recommendation: please cover all information on the copy that is not needed for the check, in particular your photo, the document number and the access number. Your name and your address must remain legible.

  • Copies of the data in backups are not deleted individually; they are overwritten in the regular backup cycle.

Ending the service and deletion

You can end the service at any time. All your data is then deleted automatically and irrevocably, in two steps:

Deleted are: your imprint page (it goes offline), your address data, your ID-card photos (on the web server and on the secure storage system), your imprint profiles and all their versions, your service requests and your account.

We keep them as a record that the processing took place and to secure our systems (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). In addition we keep a deletion record without personal data (date and number of the deleted items).

Exception (legal hold): if there is a reason that requires or entitles us to keep data, for example an open legal claim, a request from an authority or a statutory retention duty, an administrator can set a legal hold. The deletion then waits until the reason no longer applies. The data concerned is kept only for this reason (Art. 6 (1) (c) and (f) GDPR).
