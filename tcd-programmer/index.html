What we are

TCD-Programmer is a friendly developer group running since 2024. We create, learn, and improve together — with a focus on teamwork, sharing knowledge, and working on real projects.

143 active members in 2026

In 2026 we’re proud to welcome 143 active members. Thank you to everyone who contributes time, ideas, and know-how — you’re the reason the community grows.

Monthly meeting / meetup

We meet once a month to exchange our work and projects, give feedback, plan new ideas, and support each other.

What you’ll get

  • Project sharing, code reviews & helpful feedback
  • Working together on ideas, tools, and solutions
  • A welcoming space for beginners and advanced developers
